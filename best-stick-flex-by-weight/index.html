Best Hockey Stick Flex by Weight

Use body weight as a starting point, then adjust for height, skill and shooting style.

A practical weight-to-flex starting point

A common starting rule is body weight in pounds divided by two. It is only a starting point: younger players often need a softer flex, while a strong power shooter may prefer a stiffer shaft. Age and confidence matter as much as raw weight.

Fine-tuning

How to adjust your flex

  • Shooting style: quick-release players prefer a softer flex; power shooters may prefer stiffer.
  • Stick length: cutting shorter makes it feel stiffer by ~5–10 per inch.
  • Skill level: younger players almost always need a softer flex than weight alone suggests.

Common questions

Best stick flex FAQ

Is half my body weight a good flex?

Starting at half your body weight in pounds is a reasonable starting point, but youth players and quick-release shooters usually need a softer flex than this formula suggests.

Does cutting my stick change the flex?

Yes. Every inch you cut makes the stick feel noticeably stiffer. Finalize your length before choosing a flex.
