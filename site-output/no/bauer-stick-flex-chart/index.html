Buying tips

Choosing your Bauer stick

Bauer model families feel different even at the same flex. A Vapor loads more easily than a Nexus; a Supreme feels stiffer. Weight and length are the most reliable guides.

Five things to check before you buy

  • Printed flex: trust the number on the shaft, not the model name.
  • Stick length: each inch cut raises effective flex by ~5–10 points.
  • Shooting style: quick-release shooters want softer flex than power shooters.
  • Age and strength: younger players should err on the softer side.
  • Test the feel: hold the stick in your stance before cutting.

Common questions

Bauer flex FAQ

Does Bauer use the same flex across all models?

No. The same flex label can feel different between Vapor, Supreme, Nexus and Tacking models. Always check reviews for the specific model.

How do I know if my flex is too stiff?

If you cannot load the stick for snapshots without excessive effort, try a softer flex. A well-loaded stick should bend easily under your hands.
