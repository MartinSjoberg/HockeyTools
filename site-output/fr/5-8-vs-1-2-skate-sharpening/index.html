Hollow comparison

5/8 vs 1/2 Skate Sharpening

Understand the difference between a balanced hollow and a grip-first hollow.

Which one has more grip?

1/2 inch (13 mm) is deeper and usually gives more bite for stops and turns. 5/8 inch (16 mm) is shallower and often feels smoother with more glide. Neither is universally best.

In depth

5/8 inch: the balanced option

5/8 inch sits right in the sweet spot for most players. It gives enough bite to stop and turn confidently, while providing enough glide that legs don't tire quickly. It is a good default choice for forwards and recreational players.

1/2 inch: the grip-first option

1/2 inch digs deeper into the ice, giving a more aggressive bite. This is popular with defensemen who need strong edges for pivoting and with players who skate on hard, fast ice where 5/8 can feel slippery.

When to switch between them

Move from 5/8 to 1/2 if you are sliding out during stops and turns. Move from 1/2 to 5/8 if your legs feel tired or the skates feel too aggressive. Only change one variable at a time and always tell your sharpener the direction you want.

Common questions

5/8 vs 1/2 FAQ

Which hollow stops faster?

1/2 inch gives more bite, so it tends to stop faster and hold an edge better in hard turns. 5/8 inch provides more glide and a smoother feel.

Is 5/8 or 1/2 better for beginners?

5/8 inch is generally better for beginners because it is more forgiving. The extra glide and lower leg fatigue make it easier to focus on technique rather than fighting aggressive edges.

Can I alternate between 5/8 and 1/2?

Yes. Many players alternate based on ice conditions and position. Tell your sharpener exactly what you last used so the change is intentional.
