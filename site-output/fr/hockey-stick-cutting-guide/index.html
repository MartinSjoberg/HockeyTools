Before you cut

Safe cutting steps

Cutting a hockey stick is permanent. Follow these steps to avoid mistakes:

Step-by-step cutting guide

  • Mark in skates: put on skates and take a natural stance to find the right length.
  • Mark the line: use a marker or masking tape to mark the cut before cutting.
  • Measure twice: double-check the mark against your target length.
  • Use the right tool: a fine-tooth hacksaw or dedicated stick cutters works best.
  • Cut straight: keep the blade perpendicular to the shaft for a clean cut.
  • Sand smooth: lightly sand the cut edge to remove burrs.

Common mistakes

Cutting too much is the most common error. If unsure, test the uncut stick first. Remember: the stick will feel stiffer and shorter after cutting.

Common questions

Stick cutting FAQ

How much does cutting change flex?

Each inch cut increases the effective flex by roughly 5–10 points. A 2-inch cut on an 85-flex stick can feel like a 95–105 flex.

Can I add an extension if I cut too much?

Yes, extensions extend the stick and reduce effective stiffness, but they alter balance. It is better to replace the stick if the cut is excessive.

What tool should I use?

A fine-tooth hacksaw or dedicated hockey stick cutter gives the cleanest cut. Always cut slowly to avoid splintering composite.
