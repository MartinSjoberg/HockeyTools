Beginner guide

How to adjust your sharpening

If your skates slip during stops or turns, try a smaller hollow for more bite. If your legs feel tired, your skates feel stuck to the ice or you want easier speed, try a larger hollow for more glide. Change one variable at a time and always tell your sharpener what you felt.

Step-by-step: choosing your first hollow

  • Start at 5/8 inch unless you know you need extra grip.
  • Skate for 10–15 minutes and notice stops, turns and forward stride.
  • If you slip: move to 1/2 inch for more bite.
  • If it feels sticky: try 3/4 inch for more glide.
  • Wait for 3+ sessions before making another change.

Common questions

Beginner hollow FAQ

What hollow should a beginner use?

5/8 inch (16 mm) is the most common starting point for beginners because it balances grip and glide. If you struggle with slipping on stops, try 1/2 inch. If the ice feels sticky and tiring, try 3/4 inch.

Is 1/2 or 5/8 better for beginners?

5/8 inch is generally better for beginners because it is more forgiving. The extra glide and lower leg fatigue make it easier to focus on technique rather than fighting aggressive edges.

Can I go too shallow?

Yes. A very shallow hollow like 7/8 inch can make it hard to dig in for stops and sharp turns. Only try 3/4 or 7/8 once you are comfortable with edge control at 5/8.
