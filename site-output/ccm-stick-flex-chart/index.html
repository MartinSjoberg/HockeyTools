Buying tips

CCM model differences

A Tacks feels stiffer than a Ribcor; a Jetspeed sits in between. Weight and length are the most reliable guides, but feel matters too.

Five things to check before you buy

  • Printed flex: trust the number on the shaft, not the model name.
  • Stick length: each inch cut raises effective flex by ~5–10 points.
  • Shooting style: quick-release players want softer flex than power shooters.
  • Age and strength: younger players should err on the softer side.
  • Test the feel: hold it in your stance before cutting.

Common questions

CCM flex FAQ

Do CCM sticks feel different by model?

Yes. Tacks sticks tend to feel stiffer than Ribcor sticks at the same flex. Jetspeed sits between them. Always check reviews for the specific model.

How do I choose between CCM and Bauer?

Both follow similar flex ranges. Choose based on feel, price and available patterns. Use our calculator first, then test the nearest flex in both brands.
